# 6. Implementation plan: first 30, 60 and 90 days

I would build the quality process step by step over 90 days. First, I would understand the current situation, then improve the most important areas, and finally measure the results and plan further improvements.

## First 30 days — Understand and prioritise
Main focus: Understand the current situation.
- Meet management, developers, product owners, and operations to understand the product and challenges.
- Review existing tests, automation, production issues, and data quality problems.
- Identify the most important business processes and high-risk data flows.
- Agree on quality goals, responsibilities, and release criteria.
Deliverables: Current-state assessment, list of key risks, and an initial improvement plan.

Dependencies: Access to systems, test results, documentation, and team members.

Decision point: Agree with management on the highest-priority risks and improvement areas.

## Within 60 days — Improve the most important areas
Main focus: Deliver practical improvements.
- Improve and automate tests for critical workflows and integrations.
- Add checks for missing, incorrect, or duplicate data where needed.
- Define a common testing process and clear defect-handling rules.
- Establish basic quality metrics, such as production defects and test execution time.
Deliverables: Improved automated tests, agreed testing guidelines, and an initial quality dashboard.

Dependencies: Team capacity, stable test environments, and access to suitable test data.

Decision point: Review early results with management and decide where to invest further.

## Within 90 days — Establish and measure the process
Main focus: Make quality part of everyday development.
- Integrate automated tests into the development and release process.
- Ensure each team understands its quality responsibilities.
- Introduce regular reviews of risks, defects, and production incidents.
- Compare current metrics with the initial baseline and identify further improvements.
- Create a prioritised roadmap for the next three to six months.
Deliverables: A working quality process, agreed metrics, regular reporting, and a longer-term improvement roadmap.

Dependencies: Team participation, management support, and reliable metrics.

Decision point: Agree on the next priorities and improvement targets.

How I would secure buy-in
- Management: Show how better quality reduces production problems, business risk, and rework.
- Development teams: Involve them in decisions and choose improvements that make their daily work easier.
- Product and operations: Show how reliable data and fewer incidents improve customer trust and business outcomes.

How I would report progress
I would maintain a simple dashboard and a regular status report showing completed actions, open risks, key metrics, blockers, and next steps. I would review progress with management and teams regularly, using real results to decide what to improve next.

My goal: Within 90 days, establish a practical and measurable quality process that reduces important risks, supports faster feedback, and helps MIMIRO release changes with confidence.
